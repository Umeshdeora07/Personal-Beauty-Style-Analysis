Drop your song in this folder (mp3 / m4a / ogg / wav).

Then open index.html and set, near the top of the JS block:

    music: "music/your-song.mp3",

If you leave music: "" the page generates its own soft romantic
piano loop in the browser, so there is always background music.

For taniya-letter.html: name the song  alfaaz.mp3  and put it in this
folder. Nothing else to change; it starts on her first tap. Until the
file is here, the letter plays its own soft music-box tune instead.
